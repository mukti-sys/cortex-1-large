# AI Image Generator Skill

This skill teaches the prompt engineering patterns, art-direction frameworks, and quality assurance principles for producing production-ready, non-AI-looking visual assets.

## The 5-Part Art Direction Framework

To prevent "AI-generated slop" (glowing neon brains, random floating dots, blurry diffusion noise, garbled text), all prompts must be structured as an explicit creative brief:

1. **Image Type & Medium**: Explicitly declare the medium:
   - "A precision studio product photograph..."
   - "A flat minimalist 2D vector emblem..."
   - "A CNC-machined dark anodized titanium hardware component..."
2. **Core Subject & Geometry**: Describe the subject in physical, tangible terms:
   - Monolithic geometric monogram uniting the letter 'C' and a forward decision chevron.
   - Exact physical textures: bead-blasted aerospace aluminum, satin matte obsidian carbon (#090D16), razor-sharp chamfered bevels.
3. **Environment & Lighting**:
   - Softbox overhead studio lighting with diffused rim highlights.
   - Seamless, non-distracting matte dark background (#060910).
   - Zero glossy glare, zero lens flare, zero floating particle dust.
4. **Composition & Spatial Ratio**:
   - 1:1 square for app icons / avatars, perfectly centered with generous negative space.
   - 16:9 ultra-wide for repository hero banners, left-to-right balanced visual hierarchy.
5. **Negative Constraints (Mandatory Anti-Slop Filter)**:
   - "NO glowing blue neural network webs, NO random floating dots, NO clip art, NO fantasy sci-fi holograms, NO illegible pseudo-text, NO low-resolution diffusion noise."

## Logo vs Banner Guidelines

| Asset | Primary Medium | Key Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brand Logo / Avatar** | 1:1 Aspect Ratio · Physical or Flat Minimalist | Never put small prose text inside the logo mark. Must be iconic at 32x32 px. |
| **Hero Banner** | 16:9 Ultra-Wide · Studio Product / Architectural | Clear visual focus on the left, tangible hardware or product interface on the right. |
